PalCraft {VERSION} ships a portable Minecraft launcher setup so Melty can start PalCraft in one click.
It doesn't include Minecraft (Prism Launcher downloads it after you sign in with a Microsoft account that
owns Minecraft: Java Edition) or anything from Palworld (PalCraft reads the Pals from your own Palworld
install the first time it starts, into Prism/instances/PalCraft/.minecraft/palcraft on your PC).
